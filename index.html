Leaf Spring Multi-Domain Optimization

Assessed structural steel, composite, and hybrid configurations using finite element simulation and weighted Pugh decision matrix. Recommended an S-glass/epoxy-steel sandwich achieving 28% simulated weight reduction and 66% higher stiffness.

SASTRA Materials Research

Laser Shock Peening on ZE41 Mg Alloy

Investigated pulsed Nd:YAG laser shock peening on aerospace/automotive ZE41 magnesium alloy without coating. Demonstrated significant surface microstructural refinement and an ultimate tensile strength increase from 134 to 168 MPa (+25.4%).
