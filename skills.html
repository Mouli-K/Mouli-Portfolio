Standards, Regulatory Codes & GD&T

Rigorous compliance frameworks governing pressure boundaries and geometric accuracy

VALVES & PRESSURE BOUNDARIES Industrial Standard

ASME B16.34 – Valves Flanged, Threaded & Welding End

  • • Pressure-Temperature Ratings: Material class selection across Class 150, 300, 600, 900, 1500, and 2500 for severe-service fluids.
  • • Minimum Wall Thickness ($t_m$): Analytical calculation and CAD verification ensuring structural integrity against pressure burst stresses.
  • • Hydrostatic Shell & Seat Testing: Designing valve bodies to withstand minimum shell test pressures (typically $1.5\times$ rated pressure).
  • • Casting Quality Factors: Accounting for radiographic and non-destructive examination (NDE) quality factors in critical pressure walls.
GEOMETRIC ACCURACY ASME Certified

ASME Y14.5 – Geometric Dimensioning & Tolerancing (GD&T)

  • • Datum Reference Frames (DRF): Defining unambiguous primary, secondary, and tertiary functional datum planes based on assembly mating order.
  • • Feature Control Frames: Applying True Position with Maximum Material Condition (MMC) / Least Material Condition (LMC) to guarantee assembly interchangeability.
  • • Form & Orientation Control: Flatness, cylindricity, perpendicularity, parallelism, and total runout on valve stem guides and gasket sealing faces.
  • • Tolerance Stack-Up: Conducting linear worst-case and Root Sum of Squares (RSS) statistical stack-up analyses across multi-component stacks.
