Research Methodology & Simulation Matrix

Investigated a comprehensive 75-run multi-domain matrix combining parabolic taper variations, eye-joint architectures (Berlin eye vs. wrapped eye), and advanced material combinations: structural steel (65Si7), S-glass/epoxy, Carbon/epoxy, and hybrid sandwich configurations. Analyzed under static, fatigue, and harmonic dynamic loading conditions.

Recommended Engineering Concept & Decision Matrix

Applied weighted Pugh selection across mass, stress distribution, delamination resistance, torsional rigidity, and manufacturing complexity. Selected an S-glass/epoxy-steel sandwich architecture with bonded interface pads, achieving an optimal balance of energy storage, cost feasibility, 28% weight reduction, and 66% increased stiffness.
